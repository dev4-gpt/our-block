## LinkedIn

I built a multiplayer game where buying a garden hose becomes a team decision.

That is the idea behind OUR BLOCK: a small neighborhood that gets better when people work together.

Up to four friends can repair a community garden, compare supplies, approve shared purchases, grow produce and prepare a community evening. Progress is saved, so you can come back to the town you helped build.

I built it with ChatGPT Work, React, TypeScript and Three.js. Cross-device playtests pushed me to improve touch controls and connection recovery, including making sure reconnecting never automatically repeats a purchase.

There are seven visual worlds, from a forest neighborhood and snowy mountains to the Moon and Mars. The shared map and progress stay the same; the scenery, outfits and garden shelters change around them.

The most interesting part of building this was making cooperation visible: who contributed, what a purchase costs and when another person needs to approve it.

Try repairing the garden with a friend, then tell me where you got stuck:
https://our-block-1ooe.onrender.com/

It is a free-hosted prototype with four online places total, so it may need about a minute to wake.

## Handshake post

OUR BLOCK — Build a Neighborhood Together

I built OUR BLOCK with ChatGPT Work: a cooperative 3D browser game where up to four friends turn a small neighborhood into a thriving community.

Repair a broken garden, compare supplies, approve shared spending, grow produce and host a community evening. Your choices and contributions shape the same persistent town.

Choose from seven visual worlds: Forest, City, Snowy Mountains, Island & Beach, Space Station, Moon and Mars. Outfits adapt to each setting, and the space environments include enclosed garden habitats with sliding entrance doors.

Cross-device playtests helped improve touch controls and saved-seat recovery. The game also includes an accessible map and both untimed exploration and a daily-life mode.

Play: https://our-block-1ooe.onrender.com/
Source (private repository; authorized access required): https://github.com/dev4-gpt/our-block/tree/codex/cloud-hosting

Try the garden project with a friend and let me know which decision was hardest to make together.

Free-hosted prototype: four online players total across all towns; the server may take about a minute to wake.
