Build a neighborhood together in OUR BLOCK, a persistent 3D browser game for up to four friends. Repair a garden, compare supplies, approve shared spending, grow produce and host a community evening. Switch between seven world styles, from forests and beaches to the Moon and Mars, with matching outfits and space garden habitats. Built with ChatGPT Work and refined through cross-device playtests. Free-hosted prototype: four online players total; saved progress and seat recovery.
