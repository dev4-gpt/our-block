Build a neighborhood together, one smart decision at a time. OUR BLOCK is a cooperative browser game for up to four friends: investigate a broken garden, compare parts, approve shared spending, grow a market and host a community evening. Every purchase has consequences; every player can contribute. Built with ChatGPT Work and refined through cross-device playtests that improved touch controls and recovery.
