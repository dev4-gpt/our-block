OUR BLOCK — public competition release information (October 23, 2026)

Choose a nickname; no real name or birthday is needed. The server saves your town, fictional balances, inventory, actions, project progress, and durable command receipts that prevent duplicate spending. Other residents of the town can see nicknames, shared evidence, inventories, contributions, and financial outcomes. Recovery secrets and other residents' private inspection lists are excluded from client snapshots.

The browser stores a private seat recovery secret for this session. Remember my seat keeps it on this device until Forget this device is used. Forgetting clears local recovery information; it does not remove the saved town. Invitation links contain a town code, not a recovery secret. Anyone with the link may join an available seat. Share it only with intended participants.

There is no advertising, free-text chat, real purchasing, live AI service, or third-party behavioral analytics in this game. Optional frame checks remain on your device. System fonts are used. Hosting and database providers process connection and operational information under their own practices. Game diagnostics exclude credentials and recovery secrets.

Support: https://app.joinhandshake.com/profiles/r9qfrx (Aryaman Dev). Handshake may require sign-in and restrict contact. If unavailable, ask your inviter to relay the request. See /support.html. Send the release, browser/device and steps to reproduce; never send passwords, recovery tokens, banking details or browser storage. Public town links and identifiable screenshots should not be posted in public reports.

Saved towns are retained during this competition release; no inactive-town purge is scheduled. Request removal through support. The operator verifies an existing seat in-game and resolves shared ownership before removal; a nickname or town code alone is insufficient. Verified removal covers the live town, command receipts, and removal/redaction of private backup copies. Any backup awaiting treatment is barred from restoration. This manual process has no guaranteed response time. Loss of every seat recovery secret may prevent ownership verification.

Backups contain private recovery secrets and progress. The operator manually retains seven daily exports and three release exports. There is no unattended off-device backup service. A restoration must honor recorded removals. Progress is never silently deleted to make room under a quota.

This is a limited public competition release, not a certified educational or broadly validated child-directed service. Organized tests involving children require appropriate guardian permission. No birthdays or identity documents should be submitted to the game. Independent usability and learning-effectiveness studies remain uncompleted. Broader privacy/consent review and sustained operational readiness remain open.
