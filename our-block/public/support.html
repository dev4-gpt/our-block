Support and release limits

OUR BLOCK is a public competition release in testing. Fictional credits have no cash value. No real purchases, microphone, or account are required to play.

Report a problem

Contact Aryaman Dev on Handshake. Handshake may require sign-in and limit contact according to your account. If contact is unavailable, ask the person who invited you to relay the request. Do not post a private town link publicly.

Include the release identifier shown in the game, your browser/device, what you did, and what happened. Remove private messages and personal details from screenshots. Never send recovery tokens, passwords, payment information, or browser storage.

Saved-town removal

Request removal through the same support route. This is a manual shared-town request, not an instant self-service button. The operator must verify control through the existing in-game seat and resolve other residents' interests before deleting shared progress. A nickname or town code alone is not proof of ownership. Forget this device only clears local recovery information.

Progress is retained during this competition release; inactive towns are not automatically deleted. Verified removal includes live town data and its command receipts. Private backups are removed or redacted when processing a verified deletion; any copy still awaiting treatment must not be restored to service. We do not promise a response time or automatic deletion date.

Availability

The free service admits four online residents in total, across all towns. A town has four recoverable seats. Wait and reconnect if the service is full; your saved progress remains. Free hosting can sleep and may take about a minute to wake. After 90 seconds the game offers a manual retry. A reconnect never automatically repeats a purchase.

Backups are manual: seven daily copies and three release copies, retained privately. No unattended off-device backup schedule or uptime guarantee is provided. Our device checks are technical observations, not proof of learning effectiveness or universal suitability. Unity downloads remain experimental.
